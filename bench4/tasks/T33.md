# T33: Aging report for open purchase orders

Suppliers keep us waiting and we only notice when the shelf is already empty.
We want to see at a glance how long our open orders have been sitting.

`report aging --as-of YYYY-MM-DD` (defaults to today) groups every pending
order into three buckets by its age in days, i.e. from the order date up to the
as-of date: `0-7`, `8-30` and `31+`. Received and cancelled orders don't
appear at all.

Acceptance criteria:
- An order placed 7 days before the as-of date lands in `0-7`; 8 days in
  `8-30`; 30 days in `8-30`; 31 days in `31+`.
- The CLI prints all three bucket headings even when a bucket is empty, with
  each order (id, sku, qty, date) listed under its bucket. Exit 0.
- Library: `reports.order_aging(store, as_of)` accepts an ISO date string or a
  `datetime.date` and returns a dict with exactly the keys `"0-7"`, `"8-30"`,
  `"31+"`, each mapping to a list of order dicts carrying at least `id`, `sku`,
  `qty` and `date`.
