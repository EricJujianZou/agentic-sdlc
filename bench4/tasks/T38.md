# T38: Bug: order history is out of order

The order history report is not chronological. For one of our SKUs it lists the
order we placed on 20 January 2026 before the one from 5 January 2026 — that
older one was typed in back in the day when we wrote dates like `2026-1-5`.
Scrolling a longer history, orders from years back show up in the middle of
recent ones.

History should read oldest first, full stop, no matter how the date was
originally typed. Orders placed on the same day should stay in the order they
were placed.
