# T02 — Supplier lead times

Our suppliers deliver at very different speeds and we keep getting caught
out. Please track how long each one takes.

- `add-supplier` gets an optional `--lead-time DAYS` (whole days).
  Suppliers created without it have a lead time of 0.
- The value is saved with the supplier and survives restarts. In code,
  `Store.add_supplier(...)` accepts a `lead_time_days=` keyword and
  `Supplier` records carry a `lead_time_days` attribute.
- State files saved before this feature existed must keep loading; their
  suppliers simply have a lead time of 0.
- Reorder suggestions should show what we are up against: each row from
  `reports.reorder_suggestions(...)` now also includes `lead_time_days`
  for the item's supplier, so the slow suppliers stand out when deciding
  what to order first.
