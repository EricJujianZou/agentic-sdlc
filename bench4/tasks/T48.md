# T48 — Multi-step undo, and redo

Undo has saved us more than once, but one step at a time is painful, and
there is no way back when we undo one step too many.

- `undo --steps N` (default 1) undoes the N most recent changes, newest
  first. If fewer than N changes are available to undo, exit 1 with an
  error and undo nothing at all.
- New command `redo` (also `--steps N`, default 1) re-applies what was just
  undone, walking forward again in the original order: after
  `undo --steps 2`, the first `redo` brings back the older of the two
  changes, a second `redo` brings back the newest. With nothing left to
  redo, exit 1 and change nothing.
- Making any NEW change (receive, ship, add-item, an order operation, ...)
  clears the redo history: a `redo` after that exits 1 with an error and
  changes nothing.
- Like undo today, all of this must work across separate invocations of the
  CLI.
