# T31: Supplier on-time delivery report

We negotiate lead times with our suppliers but have no idea who actually honours
them. Two asks:

1. When we mark a purchase order received, let us record the day it actually
   arrived: `receive-order <id> --date YYYY-MM-DD`, defaulting to today when
   omitted. Scripts should see the arrival date on the order as `received_date`.
2. A new report, `report on-time`, showing per supplier how many of their
   received orders arrived on time, and the percentage.

Acceptance criteria:
- An order is on time when its arrival date is at most the supplier's lead time
  (in days) after the day the order was placed; arriving exactly on the last day
  of that window still counts as on time.
- The supplier for an order is the ordered item's supplier. Only received orders
  with a recorded arrival date count; pending and cancelled orders never affect
  the numbers. Suppliers with no countable orders are left out.
- CLI: `report on-time` prints one line per supplier with the supplier id, the
  on-time count out of the total, and the percentage. Exit 0.
- Library: `reports.supplier_on_time(store)` returns a list of dicts sorted by
  supplier id, each with keys `supplier_id`, `total`, `on_time`, `pct` (0-100).
