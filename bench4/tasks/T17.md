# T17 — Suppliers deliver orders in parts now

Our suppliers rarely deliver a whole purchase order at once anymore, and I
have no way to book half a delivery.

What I want:

- `ship-order ID --qty N` (API: `Store.ship_order(order_id, qty)`) records
  N units of order ID arriving: stock goes up by N and the order keeps a
  running `shipped_qty`.
- While part of the order is still outstanding the order stays `pending`.
  The moment `shipped_qty` reaches the ordered quantity, the order flips to
  `received` on its own.
- N must be at least 1 and no more than what is still outstanding on the
  order; otherwise print an error, exit 1, and change nothing.
- Only pending orders can take deliveries — booking against a cancelled or
  already-received order is an error (exit 1).
- `receive-order ID` still works and now means "the rest arrived": it books
  whatever is still outstanding in one go (stock goes up by the remainder
  only, not the full order quantity again) and marks the order received.
