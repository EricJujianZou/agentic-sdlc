# T29: Monthly revenue report

At the end of the month we want one number for what the month's completed
orders were worth:

- `reports.monthly_revenue(store, month)` for a month like `"2026-07"`
  returns `{"rows": [...], "total": ...}`. There is one row per order dated
  in that month whose status is received, with keys `id`, `sku`, `total` —
  that order's total, same math as the invoice breakdown (category discount
  and tax included, at current prices). `total` at the top level is the
  dollar sum of the row totals. Pending and cancelled orders do not count.
- CLI: `report revenue <YYYY-MM>` prints one line per counted order and
  always ends with a `Total revenue: $X.XX` line in the standard money
  format — `$0.00` when the month had nothing.
