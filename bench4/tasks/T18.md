# T18 — Supplier catalogues, and orders that know their supplier

Each of our suppliers sends us the list of SKUs they can supply. I want
that recorded, and I want placing an order to figure out the supplier for
me.

What I want:

- `catalog-add SUPPLIER SKU` (API: `Store.add_supplier_sku(supplier_id,
  sku)`) records that SUPPLIER can supply SKU. `catalog-list SUPPLIER`
  prints that supplier's SKUs, one per line. API:
  `Store.suppliers_for(sku)` returns the sorted list of supplier ids whose
  catalogue lists the SKU.
- Orders now remember who they were placed with: orders carry a
  `supplier_id`.
- `place-order` picks the supplier automatically:
  - exactly one catalogue lists the SKU -> that supplier;
  - more than one -> you must pass `--supplier ID`, otherwise it is an
    error (exit 1) and no order is created;
  - no catalogue lists it -> fall back to the item's own supplier (which
    may be none).
- `--supplier` must name a known supplier and always wins when given.
- Catalogue entries persist like everything else.
