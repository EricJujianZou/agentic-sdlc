# T20 — Show me movement, not just levels: category turnover

Stock levels tell me what is sitting still; I want to know what actually
moves. Give me units shipped out, by category, month over month.

What I want:

- `ship` gains `--date YYYY-MM-DD` (default: today) and every shipment is
  recorded — which SKU, how many, from which warehouse, on what date.
- New report: units shipped per category per month.
  API: `reports.turnover(store)` returns a mapping
  `{category: {"YYYY-MM": units, ...}, ...}`.
  CLI: `report turnover` prints one line per category+month with the unit
  count.
- The category is whatever the shipped item's category is.
- Warehouse transfers are internal moves, not shipments — they must not
  show up in turnover. Receiving stock or purchase orders doesn't count
  either; only `ship`.
