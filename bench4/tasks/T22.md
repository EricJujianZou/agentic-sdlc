# T22: Inventory valuation is off by a cent

Our accountant reconciles the stock report's total inventory value against
her spreadsheet at month end. This month the report said $8,797.69 while the
spreadsheet says $8,797.70. We re-checked every line by hand: quantity times
unit price is right on each row of the spreadsheet, and its grand total is
right. The report is the thing that's wrong, and it seems to depend on the
mix of prices — with some combinations of items the reported total comes out
a tiny fraction under or over the true amount, and occasionally a per-line
value is similarly off.

Valuation figures must come out mathematically exact, every time, for any mix
of quantities and prices. Nothing else should change: the report keeps
returning plain dollar figures exactly as before.
