# T06 — Low-stock report grouped by shelf area

When I restock I walk the room one shelf area at a time, so the
low-stock list should come grouped by category (the category support
you added), not as one flat list.

- New report function
  `reports.low_stock_by_category(store, threshold=5)` returns a dict
  mapping category name -> that category's low-stock rows (same row
  contents as the flat low-stock report: `sku`, `name`, `qty`; rows
  sorted by SKU).
- Categories with nothing running low are left out of the dict entirely.
- Items that never got a category show up under `uncategorized`.
- CLI: `report low --by-category` prints each category as a heading with
  its low items beneath it. The flat `report low` stays as-is.
