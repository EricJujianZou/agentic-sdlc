# T30: CSV prices that survive a round trip

Two long-standing annoyances with CSV and money:

- Our own exports write prices like `0.1`. Price cells in exported CSV
  should always carry exactly two decimals: `0.10`, `19.99`, `3.50`.
- Supplier files write prices however they like: `3.5`, `3.50`, `$3.50`,
  sometimes with stray spaces around the value. Import should accept all of
  those forms, in every CSV layout we support (with or without the category
  and warehouse columns). A price cell that is not money at all (`abc`)
  stays a user error (exit 1).
- Exporting and then importing into a fresh data directory must reproduce
  the items exactly: same quantities, same prices, and the same total
  valuation to the cent.
